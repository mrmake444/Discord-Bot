# Privacy Policy

**Applies to:** [mrmake] (the "Bot"), a Discord application
**Last updated:** August 14, 2026

This policy explains what information the Bot handles, why, and what control you have over it. The Bot is a free, non-commercial hobby project operated by an individual.

## 1. Information the Bot Handles

**Provided automatically by Discord when you use a command:**

- Your Discord user ID and username
- The server (guild) ID and channel ID where the command was used
- The time the command was used

**Provided by you as command input:**

- Rocket League platform and player identifier (e.g. Steam ID, Epic username, gamertag) that you supply to look up statistics

**Retrieved from third parties:**

- Publicly available Rocket League match and rank statistics associated with the identifier you supply

## 2. Information the Bot Does NOT Handle

The Bot does not read, store, or process:

- The content of your messages, other than the parameters you type into its slash commands
- Direct messages, voice data, or attachments
- Email addresses, real names, physical addresses, or phone numbers
- Passwords, payment information, or financial data

The Bot does not sell, rent, or trade any information to anyone, and does not use it for advertising or profiling.

## 3. Why This Information Is Used

Solely to respond to your command — to look up the requested statistics, format a reply, and return it to the channel you used. Aggregate, non-identifying counts (such as total commands run) may be used to monitor performance and diagnose errors.

The Bot may cache a Discord user ID alongside a linked Rocket League identifier so you do not have to re-enter it on each command. Any such data is stored on self-hosted hardware located in the United States, in a container accessible only to the operator, and is not backed up to any third-party cloud service.

Operational logs (command names, timestamps, and error messages) are retained on a short rolling basis for troubleshooting and are overwritten automatically.

If the Bot is permanently discontinued, stored data is deleted.

## 5. Third-Party Services

Your use of the Bot necessarily involves these third parties, each governed by its own privacy policy:

- **Discord Inc.** — delivers all commands and responses. See the [Discord Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy).
- **[STATS PROVIDER NAME]** — supplies Rocket League statistics. The player identifier you supply is sent to this service to perform the lookup.

The operator does not control how these services handle information.

## 6. Your Choices and Rights

- **Stop providing data:** simply stop using the Bot's commands.
- **Request deletion:** contact the operator at the address below with your Discord user ID, and any stored data associated with it will be deleted.
- **Server-wide removal:** a server administrator can remove the Bot at any time, which ends all data handling for that server.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights over your personal data (such as access, correction, or deletion). Requests can be made using the contact details below.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not directed at children under 13, and the operator does not knowingly collect information from them. Use of Discord requires meeting Discord's minimum age for your country. If you believe a child under that age has provided information through the Bot, contact the operator and it will be deleted.

## 8. Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect the systems running the Bot, including credential isolation and restricted access. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated at any time. The "Last updated" date above will change to reflect any revision. Material changes will be noted in the Bot's repository or support server where practical.
